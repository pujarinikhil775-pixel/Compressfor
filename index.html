Frequently Asked Questions

Is CompressFor completely free?

Yes, CompressFor is 100% free with no hidden charges, no watermarks, and no registration required.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All compression and conversion happens directly in your browser. Your images never leave your device.

What image formats are supported?

CompressFor supports JPG, PNG, WebP, and GIF for compression. JPG, PNG, and WebP for conversion.

How do I compress an image for a Government form?

Click the Presets tab, select Government, upload your image and it will be automatically compressed to under 200KB in JPG format.

Can I compress multiple images at once?

Yes! Simply select multiple files when uploading or drag and drop several images at once into the upload zone.

What is the Instagram preset?

The Instagram preset resizes your image to a maximum of 1080px wide and compresses it under 8MB in JPG format — perfect for posts and stories.

Our Story

Why We Built CompressFor

We were tired of uploading photos for government forms, university admissions, and Instagram posts — only to get rejected because the file was "too large." Every tool out there either uploaded our private photos to unknown servers, added ugly watermarks, or asked us to sign up just to compress one image.

So we built CompressFor — a tool that works entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device. No signup, no watermark, no limits. Just fast, free, reliable compression for whatever you need it for.

Today, CompressFor helps people compress and convert images for Instagram, government forms, university uploads, and everything in between — completely free, forever.
