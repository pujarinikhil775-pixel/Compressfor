Our Story

Why We Built CompressFor

We were tired of uploading photos for government forms, university admissions, and Instagram posts only to get rejected because the file was "too large." Every tool out there either uploaded our private photos to unknown servers, added ugly watermarks, or asked us to sign up just to compress one image.

So we built CompressFor a tool that works entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device. No signup, no watermark, no limits. Just fast, free, reliable compression for whatever you need it for.

Today, CompressFor helps people compress and convert images for Instagram, government forms, university uploads, and everything in between completely free, forever.
