What Makes a Good Online Photo Compressor?

  • Privacy — Your photos should never be uploaded to a server
  • Speed — Compression should happen in under 5 seconds
  • No watermark — The compressed image should be clean
  • No signup — You shouldn't need an account to compress a photo
  • Format support — Should support JPG, PNG and WebP at minimum
  • Presets — Should have ready-made settings for common use cases

Why Browser-Based Compression is Better

Most online photo compressors work by uploading your image to their server, compressing it there, and sending it back. This means:

  • Your private photos are stored on someone else's server
  • It's slower because of the upload and download time
  • It doesn't work without a good internet connection

CompressFor works entirely in your browser. Your photo is compressed on your own device — it never goes anywhere. This is faster, safer and works even on a slow connection.
