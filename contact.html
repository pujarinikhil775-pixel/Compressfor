Common Questions

Is CompressFor completely free?

Yes — 100% free, no hidden charges, no watermarks, no signup required ever.

Are my images uploaded to your server?

No. All compression happens in your browser. Your images never leave your device.

Which formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF for compression. JPG, PNG and WebP for conversion.

Can I compress multiple images?

Yes — drag and drop multiple images at once and compress them all in one click.
