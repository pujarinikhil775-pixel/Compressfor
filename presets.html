Why Use a Preset Instead of Manual Settings?

Every platform and form has its own idea of what a "correct" photo looks like. Instagram wants a certain width, a government form wants a specific KB limit, a messaging app wants something else entirely. Figuring this out manually means checking the requirement, guessing at a quality percentage, checking the result, and adjusting again, which adds up to a lot of trial and error for something that should take a few seconds.

A preset just skips that guessing. Pick the platform or use case you're preparing for, and the correct dimensions, format, and file size limit get applied automatically. This is especially useful for requirements people run into constantly and rarely remember exactly, whether that's a display picture for a messaging app or a photo for a professional profile.

If you're preparing for a specific government exam form rather than a general upload, the requirements tend to be stricter and more exact than a typical preset covers. This guide breaks down the specific photo and signature size for SSC, UPSC, Railway and other exam bodies, so you're not left guessing whether the Government preset here matches what your particular form actually needs.
